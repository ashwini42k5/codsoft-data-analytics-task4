# CodSoft Data Analytics Internship: Task 4, Customer Data Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project is part of the **CodSoft Data Analytics Internship**.
The goal of Task 4 was to analyze customer data to understand purchasing behavior, segment customers, identify the most valuable groups, and suggest marketing strategies based on the findings.

## 📂 Dataset
**Mall Customer Segmentation Dataset** (from Kaggle)
- 200 customer records
- Columns: CustomerID, Gender, Age, Annual Income (k$), Spending Score (1-100)

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Google Colab

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
1. **Segmentation:** grouped customers by Age (5 brackets) and Annual Income (Low/Medium/High/Very High).
2. **Behavior mapping:** plotted Annual Income against Spending Score to visually identify natural customer clusters.
3. **Value segmentation:** classified customers into High Value, High Income/Low Spender, and Low Income/High Spender groups based on income and spending thresholds.
4. **Profiling:** examined the age and gender makeup of the High Value segment.
5. **Dashboard:** combined four charts (age distribution, spending by gender, value segments, age vs spending) into a single visual summary.

## 📊 Key Findings
- **High Value customers:** ___ customers, average age ___, mostly ___.
- **High Income, Low Spenders:** ___ customers — have the budget but aren't spending much here.
- **Low Income, High Spenders:** ___ customers — loyal despite a limited budget.
- The 26-35 age group makes up the largest share of customers.
- Spending score does not differ much by gender.
- High-value customers form a visually distinct cluster separate from other income/spending groups.

## 💡 Recommended Marketing Strategies
1. **High Value customers:** offer loyalty perks and early access to new products to retain them, since they drive the most revenue.
2. **High Income, Low Spenders:** target with personalized offers or premium product highlights to convert income into spending.
3. **Low Income, High Spenders:** reward loyalty with discounts or a points system, since they already engage often despite budget limits.
4. **Age 26-35 group:** since they're the largest segment, tailor campaigns (social media ads, trend-driven products) toward this age range.

## ✅ Outcome
The analysis identifies distinct customer segments by income and spending behavior, with the High Value group standing out as the priority target for retention, and tailored strategies suggested for each other segment.
